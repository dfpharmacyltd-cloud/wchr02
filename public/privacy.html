Privacy Policy — WESTCOAST Procurement

Last updated: 22 October 2026

WESTCOAST Procurement is an internal purchasing tool of West-Coast Pharmaceutical Works Ltd., Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. It is used only by authorised employees to manage suppliers, purchase inquiries and supplier quotations.

Information we handle

Business contact details of suppliers (company name, business email, phone, address), purchase inquiries, quotations submitted by suppliers through their personal link, and the names and work emails of employees who use the tool.

Google Drive

When an administrator connects Google Drive, the tool asks only for the drive.file permission. This lets it create and read only the files and folders it creates itself (the “WESTCOAST Procurement” folder with inquiry attachments and backups). It cannot see, change or delete any other file in the Drive. Google user data is used only to store these files and is not shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Microsoft Outlook

Users may connect their own Outlook mailbox so that inquiry emails are sent from it and supplier replies can be read back into the tool.

Storage and security

Data is stored in the company’s own database. Access requires a personal login; connection tokens are stored encrypted. Data is kept as long as needed for purchasing records and can be deleted on request.

Removing access

Drive access can be removed at any time from the tool (Integrations → Disconnect) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
